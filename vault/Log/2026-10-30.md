## Bugs caught along the way
- `broadcast::try_recv` lag semantics would have silently dropped output. See [[ADR-0004 Lagging clients resync from snapshot]].
- The TERM-trap test passed for the wrong reason: SIGTERM arrived before `trap` ran, so SIGKILL was never exercised. The suite finishing in 0.07s gave it away. Fixed by waiting for an `armed` marker and asserting the elapsed time is ≥ the timeout.
- `watch::Ref` guards held across `.await` made futures `!Send`. Wrapped them in a `wait_true()` helper.

## Notes
