## Agents lost on power-off (v0.5.5)

User asked whether everything comes back after closing Hive or turning the PC off. Reading the shutdown path found a race.

| Finding / change | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Power-off sends SIGTERM to every process at once; an agent dying before hived handled its own SIGTERM was recorded `Exited` and dropped from `sessions.json` (only alive or lost sessions were stored) | isolated daemon: TERM the session processes, 0.5 s later TERM hived → restart listed only older sessions, the 2 new ones gone |
| Sessions ended by a signal other than SIGINT are stored like live ones, with `Session::last_agent` (presence kept after exit) so the relaunch resumes the agent | same repro after the fix: both listed `ended†`; `daemon::an_agent_killed_just_before_the_daemon_comes_back` fails before (store never kept it), passes after |
| ADR-0025 amended; Session Lifecycle updated | |
