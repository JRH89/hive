## v0.5.9: clickable sidebar scrollbars

| | |
|---|---|
| Report | User: "the scroll bar in the spaces tab doesn't work". |
| Cause | 0.5.7 drew scrollbars but recorded no hit area; only the wheel scrolled. |
| Fix | `draw_scrollbar` records `SidebarHit::Scrollbar { list, len, room }`; `on_mouse` ranks it above rows. Click jumps (thumb centres on pointer, `app::bar_top`), drag follows until release even off the bar (`App::drag_bar`). Selection unchanged. |
| Evidence | Test `render::tests::clicking_and_dragging_the_spaces_scrollbar_scrolls_it`; real TUI in tmux, 12 spaces at 120x40: click top of track → space1 first; drag past bottom → space5..space12. Full test/clippy/fmt green. |
